## Scientific Limitations

- N=77, 33 fallers: small sample size limits statistical power.
- Five positive events per predictor is below the traditional EPV=10 heuristic.
- Single dataset; no external validation performed.
- Limited feature set (5 predictors).
- Target semantics unresolved (cohort label vs clinical diagnosis).
- Gender coding direction unresolved.
- Fall-history leakage status unresolved.
- Hyperparameter selection via inner CV on N=77 has high variance.
- Fold-to-fold variability is substantial and expected with N=77.

## No Clinical Claim

Phase 5 does not establish clinical validity, diagnostic performance,
causal relationships, or external generalizability.
All results are exploratory and descriptive.
